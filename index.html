Who We Are

Goilala Music Association (GMA) comprises youth and experienced artists from the Goilala mountains who have a deep connection to the art of music and are currently performing and recording across Port Moresby and Central Province. We have come to realize that music is a powerful tool for change — not only mentally, but in all aspects of life — which is why we are now using music to fight drug abuse, GBV, law & order issues, and to promote education, peace and culture.

Proudly participated in Central Music Arts & Culture Festival (CMACF) in Bereina 2026 funded by Hon. Governor Madam Rufina Peter.
